Quick demo

ArticleCache: Recent articles are pre-loaded by the background process. A recent article should normally give a HIT . An article older than 14 days, such as Article #20, should give a MISS .

CommentCache: The first request should be MISS , and the second request should be HIT .

Posting a comment invalidates that article's comment cache, so the next read becomes a MISS again.

LRU: The CommentCache keeps comments for a maximum of 30 recently accessed articles. When the cache is full and another article is accessed, the Least Recently Used article group is removed.

LRU demonstration

First load comments for Article 1 → 30. This fills the CommentCache to its maximum of 30 groups.



After the cache contains 1 → 30, Article 1 is the least recently used group.

Now manually open Article #31. Its comments will enter the cache, so Article 1 should be removed.
